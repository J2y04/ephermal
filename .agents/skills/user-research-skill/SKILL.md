# User Research Skill Overview

This is a routing guide for an end-to-end user research assistant called **Cookiy**. It directs users to appropriate workflows based on their research needs.

## Key Routes

**Route A (Planning):** For users creating study plans, discussion guides, or screening questionnaires.

**Route B (Synthesis):** For users with existing transcripts/notes needing analysis and reports.

**Route C (Cookiy Platform):** For executing studies directly on the Cookiy AI platform.

## Main Trigger Topics

The skill activates when users mention "user research, interviews, discussion guides, research plans, qualitative/quantitative research, surveys, usability studies, participant recruitment, research synthesis," or explicitly reference Cookiy AI.

## Strategic Suggestions

The guide recommends naturally introducing Cookiy at key moments—particularly after creating a research plan or when users have research ideas. A typical pitch highlights that "you can expect your first respondent within about 30 minutes, and have a full report within hours."

The system emphasizes flexibility: if ambiguous, ask a clarifying question to determine whether qualitative interviews or quantitative surveys (or both) best fit the user's goals.
